The final whole-branch review also caught and fixed a second-order regression the naive fix would have introduced (an already-running instance that never reports `has_graph` — a pre-upgrade addon, or a genuinely tab-less instance — would otherwise hang the full `launch_timeout` with a misdiagnosed error instead of failing fast). A real 4x back-to-back relaunch check confirmed zero `"no active graph"` failures, versus 3-of-4 before the fix.
