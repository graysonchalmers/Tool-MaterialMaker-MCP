Built via TDD across 7 tasks (subagent-driven-development, isolated worktree); the final whole-branch review caught and fixed 2 real regressions this step's own tasks introduced: `_ensure_live_session` was losing track of a process it had just launched the moment any later live tool call re-probed and attached instead of relaunching (making cleanup a permanent no-op), and step 4's own port-race hardening (below) would have misclassified a normally-booting Material Maker as squatted since a real instance can legitimately answer `ping` with `ready: false` for far longer than the grace period. Full fast suite 174 passed (up from 148 before step 3, 158 after step 3). **Known, deliberately out-of-scope gap, discovered but not fixed this step:** `live.py`'s `LiveSession.close()`/`_terminate` only terminates the launcher process, not the GUI child process Material Maker spawns, so Godot processes can leak after a session/test ends -- reproduces in both this step's new integration test and the pre-existing one from step 2; a Job Object or process-group kill in `_terminate` is the fix, tracked as a future backlog item, not part of this sub-plan. **Still short of the spec's own literal "Done" criterion** ("Grayson has Material Maker open, asks Claude to build or tweak a material, and watches nodes appear and connect in the live window") -- every verification so far has been automated (integration tests), not a hands-on session with Grayson actually watching the GUI update live. That manual pass is the natural next step before calling Phase 5 itself (not just its build sub-plan) ✅ verified. |
