# t09_marbled_silt - Marbled silt

_Category: terrain. Open the graph: `cookbook/terrain/t09_marbled_silt.ptex`._

Warm, flowing mineral sediment -- dried silt with soft rust and umber veining, structurally distinct from every plate/crack/cell material in this set.

## Recipe

This slot was repurposed after three prior passes (soft fbm, hard-thresholded fbm, and finally a `dry_earth` voronoi-plate re-base) all converged on some flavor of cracked-plate or scattered-cell topology, which made it read as a sibling of t05 ice, t08 pebbles, and the masonry family (s07-s11). Grayson's call: drop plates and cells entirely and use `fbm` for the one thing it is genuinely good at that this cookbook had zero examples of -- turbulence-driven flowing veins.

Donor is `crocodile_skin` (same as t07), retyping `voronoi_0` to `fbm`. Unlike t07's Cellular 4 clump basis, this material uses `noise=1` (Perlin), a smooth continuous basis with no cell edges at all, and pushes `folds=3` -- the turbulence/ridge lever that folds the noise field back on itself into flowing veins and swirls instead of plain soft blobs. `iterations=5` and `persistence=0.5` match t07's own values for a moderate amount of fine detail without competing with the folds; `scale_x`/`scale_y=4` (lower than t07's 6) so individual veins read as broad, room-scale swirls rather than tight repeating clumps.

Palette is warm tan/sand as the majority tone, with darker rust/umber staining picked out where the folded value swings low or high. Every gradient stop is spaced at least 0.15 apart with smooth linear interpolation -- no hard thresholds anywhere -- so the veins read as soft flowing mineral stains, not crack lines. Roughness is matte with only subtle variation. `normal_map` uses `param4=0` (the standing flat-normal fix) with a modest `param1=0.25` so the veins swell gently rather than carving sharp ridges.

Pitfall specific to this material: thresholding or hard-edging any noise field here recreates the crack-network look this repurpose exists to avoid. If the render shows veins that are too subtle, the fix is raising `folds` or narrowing the gradient's transition bands slightly, not adding a hard mask.

## Subgraph structure

Grouped per the "Grouping into subgraphs" lever in `docs/AUTHORING.md`, the same `crocodile_skin`-donor template as `t07_forest_floor`:

- **Silt Pattern** -- `VeinColor`, `SiltVeins` (retyped to `fbm`, Perlin basis with `folds=3`). Exposed: `Vein scale` (`SiltVeins.scale_x`), `Vein color` (`VeinColor.gradient`).
- **Surface Finish** -- `SiltRoughness`, `SiltHeight`, `SiltNormal`. Exposed: `Roughness` (`SiltRoughness.gradient`), `Vein relief` (`SiltNormal.param1`).

`NonMetallic` (the untouched metallic-0 scalar, feeding Material port 1 directly) stays top-level, matching the same precedent used elsewhere for untouched single-purpose scalar nodes.
